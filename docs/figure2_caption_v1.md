**Figure 2. Thermal expansion of periodic single-crystal copper from 300 to 800 K.** (a) Mean lattice parameter and (b) relative volume change with respect to 300 K. Each point summarizes three independently initialized production trajectories. Error bars represent two-sided 95% Student-t confidence intervals with two degrees of freedom and may be smaller than the markers. Relative volume changes were calculated from the directly averaged cell volumes and normalized separately within each repeat before aggregation. The zero value and zero interval at 300 K follow from this normalization. Connecting lines are guides to the eye, not fitted models.
